Privacy Policy for Turnward Compass

Last updated: 21 September 2026

The short version. Turnward Compass works out which way a sacred site lies from where you are standing. Your location is used on your phone to do that maths and is never sent to us or anyone else. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics. The one thing that does reach the internet is the advert at the bottom of the compass screen, and that is explained in full below.

Who this is from

Turnward Compass is made by Leeknboat. If you have a question about privacy, write to support@tasklio.com.

Your location

The app asks for your location so it can calculate the direction and distance to the site you have chosen. That calculation happens entirely on your phone.

  • Your location is never transmitted to us or to any server.
  • It is saved on your phone so the compass keeps working offline, and so you are not asked again every time you open the app.
  • You can refuse the permission and type a position by hand instead. The app works exactly the same way.
  • You can erase it at any time: Settings → Forget my data. You can also revoke the permission in your phone's own settings.

What is stored on your phone

All of the following stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app or use Forget my data:

  • Your last known position
  • Which sacred site you have chosen
  • Your distance units and light or dark theme preference

Advertising

The app shows a single small advert at the bottom of the compass screen, supplied by Google AdMob. Adverts are how the app is paid for. This is the only part of the app that communicates over the internet, and Google — not us — receives the following:

What Google receivesWhy
Your IP address Choosing adverts, measurement, and fraud prevention. An IP address indicates roughly which region you are in.
Advertising ID and similar device identifiers Choosing adverts and measurement. You can reset or delete this in your phone's settings.
Interactions with the advert Measuring whether an advert was seen or tapped.
Diagnostic information Detecting faults and fraudulent activity.

The precise location the compass uses is not part of this. It is never passed to the advertising system. Google may infer an approximate region from your IP address, which is a separate and much coarser thing.

Google's own explanation of how it handles this data is at business.safety.google/privacy.

Your choices about advertising

Where local law requires it — including the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom — the app shows Google's consent form the first time you open it, and no advert is requested unless you agree. You can change your answer later under Settings → Advert privacy choices.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID from your phone's settings, usually under Settings → Privacy → Ads.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no email address collected
  • No analytics or usage tracking of our own
  • Nothing sold or shared with data brokers
  • No advertising that covers the compass, and no pop-up or full-screen adverts

Children

Turnward Compass is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect anything from them. Adverts are requested at Google's most restrictive content rating.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it, and the updated version will appear at this address.
